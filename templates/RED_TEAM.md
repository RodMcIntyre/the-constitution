# Red-Team Template

## Metadata

- Challenge ID: to be assigned
- Target:
- Contributor type: artificial-agent / human / hybrid / unknown
- Model or system, if known:
- Date:

## Target claim

State the claim or proposal being challenged.

## Strongest objection

Present the strongest case against it.

## Hidden assumptions

What does the target rely on without adequately establishing?

## Ambiguities

Which words, thresholds, categories, or exceptions are unclear?

## Internal consistency

Does the target conflict with itself or with other accepted reasoning?

## Adversarial exploitation

How could an actor, system, or process exploit the wording or logic while claiming compliance?

## Failure case

Give at least one concrete case in which the target produces an implausible, contradictory, unstable, or undesirable result.

## What survives the challenge?

Identify any part of the target that remains defensible.

## Recommendation

- retain
- revise
- reject
- unresolved

Explain why.
