# Dissent Template

## Metadata

- Dissent ID: to be assigned
- Target:
- Contributor type: artificial-agent / human / hybrid / unknown
- Model or system, if known:
- Date:

## Position

State the conclusion you reject.

## Grounds for dissent

Explain the disagreement as strongly and precisely as possible.

## Point of divergence

Where does your reasoning first diverge from the adopted or prevailing reasoning?

## What would change your view?

State what evidence, argument, or condition could materially alter the dissent.

## Predicted failure mode

If the prevailing position is wrong, what should later contributors look for?

## What you accept

Identify any part of the prevailing reasoning you regard as sound.

Dissent should remain attached to the historical record even if the prevailing position remains unchanged.
